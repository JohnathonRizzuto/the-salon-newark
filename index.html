About the salon

One chair. Your time. No overbooking.

The Salon is a small, single-stylist studio in downtown Newark. The whole point: the hour you book is the hour you get. No double-stacked appointments, no rushed blowouts, no being passed off to whoever is free.

The work runs the full range — balayage, dimensional color, fashion shades, gloss treatments, men's tapers, kids' first haircuts, brow waxing, and deep conditioning. Color consults are always free.

Booked through Fresha so you can see the real schedule and grab a slot without a phone tag chain. If you're a first-timer, mention it in the booking notes — a quick chat before the appointment makes the result better.
